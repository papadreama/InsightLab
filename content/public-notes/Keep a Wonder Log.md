This week, keep a log of moments during which you feel a sense of wonder. Try not to think too much about whether it's "truly" wonder - if it comes to you, jot it down! Then, in your journal you can think about whether it's active wonder or deep wonder and how it felt, how it changed your mood, how it colored your day.
