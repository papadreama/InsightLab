# Attention/Concentration

- Significant improvement in orienting and executive control skills following the mindfulness intervention was noted, however, no changes in alerting attentional skills were detected. [@becerraCanSpecificAttentional2017] 

- Participants in Attention Training Technique and Mindful Self-Compassion showed significant increase in attention flexibility [@haukaasRandomizedControlledTrial2018].

- The meditation group demonstrated significant pre post decreases in both distractive and ruminative thoughts/behaviors compared with the control group [@jainRandomizedControlledTrial2007]

- Improved working memory and attention [@meiklejohnIntegratingMindfulnessTraining2012]

# Stress & Anxiety

- Thirty-three of 40 and 25 of 34 studies showed significant decreases in anxiety and stress respectively; 22 of 24 showed an increase in mindfulness. [@bamberMindfulnessbasedMeditationDecrease2016]

- Overall summary effect (reduced anxiety) was 0.56 across 25 primary studies. Recommend testing interventions that fit student needs with minimum of eight sessions [@bamberEffectsMindfulnessMeditation2019]

- Depressive symptoms, anxiety, and stress significantly reduced [@falsafiRandomizedControlledTrial2016].

- Short-term reduction in stress [@grayEffectivenessBriefMindfulnessBased2018]

- Participants in both groups showed significant reductions in symptoms of anxiety and depression [@haukaasRandomizedControlledTrial2018]

# Wellbeing

- The relationships between **trait mindfulness** and the **Big Five** all reached statistical significance in the expected direction: openness to experience, conscientiousness, extraversion, agreeableness, and neuroticism (negative correlation) [@banfiMetaanalysisTraitMindfulness2022]

- At **six-year follow-up**, students receiving mindfulness training reported increased well-being. Furthermore, they reported greater increases in the trajectory of dis- positional mindfulness and problem-focused coping along with greater decreases in the trajectory of avoidance-focused coping [@devibeSixyearPositiveEffects2018].

- Improved sleep quality [@grayEffectivenessBriefMindfulnessBased2018]

- Mentors who participated in mindfulness training reported significantly higher **mentor satisfaction**, greater increases in empathy, and greater decreases in autonomy [@foukalMindfulnessMentoringSatisfaction2016]

- Mindfulness training could be an effective component of a wider student mental health strategy [@galanteMindfulnessbasedInterventionIncrease2017]

- Improved life satisfaction [@dvorakovaPromotingHealthyTransition2017]

- Personal training in mindfulness skills can increase teachers’ sense of well-being and teaching self-efficacy, as well as their ability to manage classroom behavior and establish and maintain supportive relationships with students. [@meiklejohnIntegratingMindfulnessTraining2012]

- Students reported an increased ability to cope with a variety of stressors, decreased reactivity, and enhanced resilience specifically concerning mental health challenges. [@nardiStudentsExperiences8week2022]
