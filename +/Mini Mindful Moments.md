### Quick Body-Scan


---

Here are some quick mindfulness activities you can do in just a few minutes:  

### 1. **Three Deep Breaths**  
Take three slow, deep breaths. Focus on the sensation of the air entering and leaving your body. Let your shoulders relax with each exhale.  

### 2. **Body Scan (2-Minute Version)**  
Briefly scan your body from head to toe. Notice any areas of tension or relaxation without judgment.  

### 3. **Five Senses Exercise**  
Identify:  
- 5 things you can see  
- 4 things you can feel  
- 3 things you can hear  
- 2 things you can smell  
- 1 thing you can taste  

### 4. **Mindful Observation**  
Pick an object near you (a plant, a mug, or even your hand). Look at it closely, noticing details like texture, color, and shape.  

### 5. **Pause and Feel Your Feet**  
Bring your attention to the sensation of your feet on the ground. Feel the connection and notice the support beneath you.  

### 6. **One-Minute Gratitude**  
Take a moment to think of one thing you're grateful for and why. Let yourself feel appreciation.  

### 7. **Box Breathing**  
Inhale for 4 counts, hold for 4 counts, exhale for 4 counts, and hold for 4 counts. Repeat 2–3 times.  

### 8. **Mindful Stretch**  
Do a gentle stretch, such as reaching your arms overhead or rolling your shoulders. Pay attention to the sensations in your muscles as you move.  

### 9. **Mindful Listening**  
Pause and listen to the sounds around you. Try to identify as many as you can without judgment.  

### 10. **Loving-Kindness Mini-Practice**  
Silently repeat a simple phrase like:  
- "May I be safe."  
- "May I be happy."  
- "May I be healthy."  

Then extend these wishes to someone else.  

These practices are versatile and can be done in any setting, making them perfect for busy schedules.
