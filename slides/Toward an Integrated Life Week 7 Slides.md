### From the Literature

+ Wonder is an intrinsically motivated exploration that expands and enriches the quest for knowledge to new dimensions.[^1]
+ Schinkel[^2] contrasts two main types of wonder:
	- **Active Wonder**: Like curiosity, it involves a drive to explore, investigate, and explain. It is motivated by a "not-yet-knowing" and seeks to resolve that state by finding answers and gaining knowledge.
	- **Deep Wonder**: It is characterized by a sense of mystery, an acceptance that some things may never be fully understood, and an appreciation of the inherent wonder of the object, even without a full explanation.



[^1]: Bazhydai, M., & Westermann, G. (2020, June 20). _From Curiosity, to Wonder, to Creativity: A Cognitive Developmental Psychology Perspective_.

[^2]: Schinkel, A. (2017). The Educational Importance of Deep Wonder. _Journal of Philosophy of Education_, _51_(2), 538–553
