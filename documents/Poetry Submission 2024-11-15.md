## Absolve

come down at dusk  
when the sea breathes  
so gently it glistens   
like oil in the amber light    
while the sun sets   
behind the last snowy patch   
on the mountain above  
where the bard  
and prophet Orpheus  
made his home  

come down and wade      
into the water  
to be baptized again  
to let the salt cure    
the regret of lost days  
and absolve yourself  
of the sin of forgetting  
your way home  

summer by summer   
the seabed has changed  
stones of gray chambray   
or black with white veins  
and wide plots of seaweed  
stained like dark coffee  
shift with the churning tides  

tread your path with care  
across the slick stones  
and over the seaweed plots  
out into the great expanse  
then float on your back until    
it all starts to feel familiar  
the silence underneath  
the smell of salt and sand  

wait for the moon to rise   
and listen as whispered   
notes of forgiveness   
roll down like pebbles  
from the mountain tomb  
of Orpheus and his lyre  
saying 

*return home*   
*and take up the tune*

## Begin Again

light breaks 
the blueberry sky    
a new day opens   
with a quiet overture    

i take my coffee  
by the window

the light starts to sing  
and the wet grass shimmers   
like a million strands of tinsel   
each blade like a slender saint  
robed in green and waiting  
for the weight of dewdrops   
that formed in the night  
to dissolve in the warmth   
of morning

in this sweet aloneness  
i let my worries condense  
clear as drops of dew  
and faithfully worship    
the dappled light of morning

i whisper a prayer   
to every greenly saint  
that dewdrops might dissolve  
that i might begin again

## The Conversation

palms rustle at daybreak  
and tiny songbirds  
greet and scatter  

the breeze carries  
a wordless exchange  
between the ocean  
and the mountains

all i do is  
listen

a moment of stillness  
is my silent prayer  
to be unburdened  
by words  

to receive the conversation  
without hopes or expectations
